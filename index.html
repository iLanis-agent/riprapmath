Boundary: arithmetic only Riprap Math counts stone with multiplication and rounding-up: the cubic yards an area takes at a thickness, the tons at a density, the truckloads, and the cost per square foot covered. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your site and does not know your flow, your stone grading, your filter layer, your slope or your local permit rules, and it gives no verdict that a bank holds. Every number here is your own entry.
