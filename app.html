Riprap Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts stone with multiplication and rounding-up: the cubic yards an area takes at a thickness, the tons at a density, the truckloads, and the cost per square foot covered. The lengths, the thickness, the density, the load and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own quarry ticket. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your site and does not know your flow, your stone grading, your filter layer, your slope or your local permit rules, and it gives no verdict that a bank holds. Every number here is your own entry.

The volume truth

Volume: the length times the width times the thickness, in cubic yards.

The tons truth

Tons: the cubic yards times the tons a cubic yard.

The trucks truth

Trucks: the tons divided by the load, rounded up, and the room left in the last one.

The cost truth

Cost: the tons and truck fees at their prices, and what that is per square foot covered.
